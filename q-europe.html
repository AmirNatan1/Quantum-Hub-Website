q2scale

Q2Scale, with EIT Global Outreach

Q2Scale is a two-month hybrid scale-up programme for European startups in automotive, energy, supply chain and sustainability, run with EIT Global Outreach. It exists to turn technical readiness into global market traction through real-world validation and POC partnerships. Two cohorts have run.

Institutional partners

EIT — the European Institute of Innovation and Technology — is Europe's largest innovation network, spanning 488 higher education institutions, 1,387 companies including SMEs, and 240 cities, regions and NGOs. Its Global Outreach programme scales European startups into new markets through hubs including Silicon Valley, Israel, the UK, Japan and Brazil.

EBN, the European Business and Innovation Centre Network, connects business-support organisations across Europe and manages the EU|BIC certification. Jumpstarter supports early-stage teams in validating an idea and building a business model around it.
