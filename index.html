who we are

A matchmaker with a workshop.

Quantum-Hub is the shared innovation arm for five corporate groups operating across Israel and Europe. Across Automotive, Logistics, Industry 4.0 and Energy, we scout globally against partner-defined needs, then prove the strongest matches in a POC environment built from working industrial assets rather than a lab.

industries we cover

One operating network across four sectors.

Automotive

Mobility, sensing, safety and vehicle technology, tested against partner use cases on instrumented vehicles.

Logistics

Warehousing, tracking, routing and fleet operations, across the full length of the partner value chain.

Industry 4.0

Automation, robotics and industrial data for facilities that cannot afford to stop.

Energy

Efficiency, resilience, storage, process technology and lower-impact energy systems.

spark — poc runway programme

Equity-free. Industry-backed. Built to commercialise.

SPARK runs MVP+ startups through a thirteen-week POC built around a use case chosen by a partner company. No equity. No participation fee. Eleven cohorts have run to date.

q-europe

The same method, on European ground.

Q-Europe connects corporations and startups across Europe, co-led with Grand Automotive and delivered with EIT Global Outreach. Its Q2Scale programme has run two cohorts, launching at VivaTech in Paris and closing at Slush in Helsinki.
