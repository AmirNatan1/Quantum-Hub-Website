industries we cover · technology we seek

Four sectors, one connected playground.

Our partners span the length of an industrial value chain: subcontract manufacturing, vehicle assembly and importing, road haulage, warehousing, refining, data centres and construction. A technology proven at one point on that chain can usually be tested at several others.

Automotive

Partner operations cover assembly, importing, distribution, car carriers, fleets and after-sales — so a vehicle technology can be tested at whichever point of that chain it actually belongs.

Logistics

Road haulage, cargo terminals, logistics centres, warehousing and last-mile delivery operate daily across the partner group, which makes them unusually testable environments.

Industry 4.0

Production lines, refineries and data centres carry a hard constraint: they cannot be paused for an experiment. POCs are designed around that.

Energy

The partner group produces, stores, distributes and consumes energy at industrial scale, including hydrogen production capacity of up to 8 tons per hour.
