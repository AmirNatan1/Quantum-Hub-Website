corporate groups

Five corporate groups. One test environment.

Quantum-Hub is the shared innovation arm for five industrial groups. Their combined operations run from subcontract manufacturing and vehicle assembly through importing, car carriers, road haulage, cargo terminals and warehousing, to refining, data centres and construction. That span is the reason a POC can usually be placed where it genuinely belongs.

founding partners

Taavura–Livnat Group

The leading mobility, logistics, energy and services holding group in Israel, with 100+ subsidiaries. Operations include data centres, logistics centres, road haulage, contact centres, earth moving and mining, cranes and heavy lift, agricultural products, vehicle importing, fashion and e-commerce.

Talcar

Exclusive importer and distributor of KIA, SsangYong and SERES vehicles in Israel. The related family business includes Ray Shipping, a tonnage supplier in the car carrier market.

strategic partners

Hyundai Motor Group

A global corporation with a value chain built on leading automobile brands — Hyundai, Kia, Genesis and IONIQ — alongside steel and construction, logistics, finance, IT and services. Our Advanced Testing Vehicle Platform was built with Hyundai.

VDL Group

A global industrial family business in subcontracting, car assembly, and buses and coaches, headquartered in Eindhoven. 104 companies, 20 countries, 17,000 employees.

Bazan Group

The largest refinery and petrochemical complex in Israel, producing petroleum products, polymers and aromatic compounds. Bazan is also Israel's largest hydrogen producer, with capacity of up to 8 tons per hour.

european lead partner

Grand Automotive

Importer and distributor of 13 car brands across 13 European countries, covering passenger cars, light commercial vehicles and spare parts, with retail sites for vehicle sales and servicing alongside mobility services, financing, insurance and used car sales. Grand Automotive co-leads Q-Europe.

The value chain is the point.

A technology that proves itself on a truck fleet can often be re-scoped for a warehouse, a production line or a terminal without leaving the group. Thirty-four portfolio companies have moved through this network, and 29 implementations have gone group-wide.
