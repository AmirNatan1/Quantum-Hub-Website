the case for a poc

Make uncertainty smaller before rollout gets bigger.

A POC is not a demonstration. It is a controlled reduction of risk: one unknown isolated, measured against a baseline, and answered clearly enough to justify a decision either way.

the evidence engine

A disciplined trial turns one unknown at a time.

The sequence protects the operation from a vague experiment and protects the startup from a moving target.

01

Isolate the risk

Name the single thing that has to be true. A test that measures four variables at once answers none of them.

02

Design the test

Agree the baseline, the metric, the site, the duration and the threshold that counts as success — before anything is installed.

03

Run and measure

Quantum-Hub runs the test on partner assets, with a project team accountable for execution rather than for enthusiasm.

04

Choose the next move

Scale, adjust or stop. All three are legitimate results, and an early stop is cheaper than a slow one.

the decision lens

A useful POC earns a clear answer.

operational fit

Measure the before and after.

Without a baseline there is no result, only an impression. We record the current state before the technology arrives, so the comparison afterwards is arithmetic rather than opinion.

the operation

Startup responsibilities

Some POCs end in a commercial agreement.

GenCell moved from POC engagement with VDL Group to a commercial agreement. A driver-behaviour software company progressed to commercial agreement across several Taavura–Livnat operating companies. Both started as one scoped test against one defined need.
